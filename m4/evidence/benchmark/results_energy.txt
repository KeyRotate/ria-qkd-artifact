Cross-check (back-to-back energy blocks):

Interpretation note:
  The canonical values above come from isolated, PA5-bounded operation
  windows. The block values are aggregate measurements over repeated
  operations and their surrounding loop/marker/measurement interval; they
  are not directly interchangeable with isolated-operation values. The source
  run did not record a decomposition of the 20.7% decapsulation difference,
  so the manuscript uses only the isolated-operation values (2.17 mJ and
  53.4 mJ). Both measurements are retained for provenance.
