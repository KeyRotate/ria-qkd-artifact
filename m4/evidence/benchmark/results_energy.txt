STM32F407 168MHz (PLL HSE 8MHz) energy measurements via PPK2
Device: STM32F407G-DISC1 MB997E, Cortex-M4 r0p1, 168MHz, 3.3V
Firmware: ml-kem-512 + ml-dsa-44, dec_ok=1 sign_ok=1 done=1

Cycle counts (DWT, clock-independent):
  ML-KEM-512 decap: min=max=avg=881252 cycles = 5.245ms @168MHz
  ML-DSA-44 sign:  min=5852905 (34.8ms) avg=17012720 (101.3ms) max=54650113 (325.3ms)

Energy per operation (cycle-loop, PA5-bounded, 100kHz sampling):
  ML-KEM-512 decap: 20 samples, avg dur 5.25ms, avg I 125.2mA, E=2168 uJ (2.17 mJ)
  ML-DSA-44 sign:   20 genuine signing windows, avg E=48487 uJ (48.5 mJ), range 16091-156428 uJ
                    avg I ~145mA
  Original duration-only classifier: 22 windows, avg E=53418 uJ (53.4 mJ);
                                    two post-loop idle-indicator pulses were included.

Cross-check (back-to-back energy blocks):
  decap block 200x: total 523199 uJ -> 2616 uJ/op
  sign block   20x: total 1057377 uJ -> 52869 uJ/op

Interpretation note:
  The canonical values above come from isolated, PA5-bounded operation
  windows. The original duration-only classifier also captured two
  post-loop idle-indicator pulses; its 53.4 mJ mean is retained only as
  unfiltered provenance, while 48.5 mJ is the corrected mean for the 20
  genuine signing windows. The block values are aggregate measurements over
  repeated operations and their surrounding loop/marker/measurement interval;
  they are not directly interchangeable with isolated-operation values. The
  source run did not record a decomposition of the 20.7% decapsulation
  difference, so the manuscript uses only the isolated-operation values.

Reproducibility:
  `ppk2_offline_decode.py --benchmark --csv ppk2_sign_windows.csv
  m4bench/ppk2_cap_log.bin` re-decodes the raw PPK2 capture with the
  ppk2-api==0.9.2 default calibration constants (no PPK2 device or network is
  required) and reproduces the 20 genuine signing windows whose mean energy is
  48.5 mJ (48,487 uJ). `ppk2_sign_windows.csv` lists every PA5 window in the
  benchmark capture with its start time, duration, average/peak current, and
  energy, including the region/duration/current classification used for the
  per-signature mean.
